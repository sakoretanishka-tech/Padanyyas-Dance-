OUR JOURNEY

About Padanyyas

A journey of rhythm, expression and dedication.

Padanyyas Kathak Nrityalaya is devoted exclusively to the classical Indian dance form of Kathak.

The academy aims to provide students with a strong foundation in the traditional aspects of Kathak while nurturing their individual artistic expression.

Students are guided through systematic learning, regular practice and examination-oriented preparation while developing an appreciation for the cultural heritage of Indian classical dance.

THE ART FORM

Discover Kathak

Where rhythm meets expression.

Kathak is one of the major classical dance traditions of India. It combines intricate footwork, rhythmic patterns, graceful movements and expressive storytelling.

At Padanyyas Kathak Nrityalaya, students are introduced to the discipline and beauty of this classical art form through systematic training.

Taal & Rhythm

Understanding rhythm and musical structure.

Tatkar & Footwork

Building precision, balance and control.

Abhinaya

Expressing emotions through movement.

Grace & Technique

Developing elegance and stage presence.
